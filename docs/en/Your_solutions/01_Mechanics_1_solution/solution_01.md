---

## 1. Projectile Motion

Given:

$$
v_0 = 100 \text{ m/s}, \quad \theta = 37^\circ
$$

---

# Step 1 — Decompose velocity

Horizontal component:

$$
v_{0x} = v_0 \cos\theta
$$

Vertical component:

$$
v_{0y} = v_0 \sin\theta
$$

---

# Step 2 — Equations of motion

Horizontal motion (no acceleration):

$$
x(t) = v_0 \cos\theta \cdot t
$$

Vertical motion (gravity acts downward):

$$
y(t) = v_0 \sin\theta \cdot t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2
$$

---

# Step 3 — Time of flight

At landing:

$$
y = 0
$$

Solve:

$$
v_0 \sin\theta \cdot T - \frac{1}{2}gT^2 = 0
$$

$$
T = \frac{2 v_0 \sin\theta}{g}
$$

---

# Step 4 — Maximum height

At top:

$$
v_y = 0
$$

Use:

$$
v_y^2 = v_{0y}^2 - 2gH
$$

$$
H = \frac{(v_0 \sin\theta)^2}{2g}
$$

---

# Step 5 — Range

$$
R = v_{0x} \cdot T
$$

$$
R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
$$
