# Step 1 — Vector addition

$$
\vec{v}_{total} = \vec{v}_{boat} + \vec{v}_{river}
$$

---

# Step 2 — Direction

To go straight across:

$$
\sin\theta = \frac{v_{river}}{v_{boat}}
$$

---

# Step 3 — Time

Only vertical component matters:

$$
v_y = v_{boat} \cos\theta
$$

$$
t = \frac{200}{v_y}
$$
