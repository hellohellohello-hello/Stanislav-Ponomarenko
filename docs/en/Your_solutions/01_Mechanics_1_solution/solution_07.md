$$
x(t), y(t)
$$

---

# Step 1 — Eliminate parameter

Solve one equation for \(t\), substitute into the other.

---

# Step 2 — Velocity

$$
\vec{v} = (x'(t), y'(t))
$$

---

# Step 3 — Acceleration

$$
\vec{a} = (x''(t), y''(t))
$$

---

# Step 4 — Check constancy

If:

$$
\vec{a} = \text{constant}
$$

→ motion is uniformly accelerated
