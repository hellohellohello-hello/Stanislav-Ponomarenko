## 10. Force Field and Power

Given:

$$
\vec{r}(t)
$$

---

# Step 1 — Velocity

$$
\vec{v} = \frac{d\vec{r}}{dt}
$$

---

# Step 2 — Acceleration

$$
\vec{a} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}
$$

---

# Step 3 — Momentum

$$
\vec{p} = m\vec{v}
$$

---

# Step 4 — Force

$$
\vec{F} = m\vec{a}
$$

---

# Step 5 — Power

$$
P = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}
$$

---
