## 1. Gravitational Dependence (Pendulum)

Given:

$$
T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
$$

---

# Step 1 — Period on the Moon

On the Moon:

$$
g_{moon} = \frac{g}{6}
$$

Substitute into formula:

$$
T_{moon} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g/6}}
$$

$$
T_{moon} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{6L}{g}}
$$

$$
T_{moon} = \sqrt{6} \cdot T_{Earth}
$$

---

# Step 2 — Length for T = 1 s

Rearrange formula:

$$
T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
$$

Square both sides:

$$
T^2 = 4\pi^2 \frac{L}{g}
$$

Solve for L:

$$
L = \frac{gT^2}{4\pi^2}
$$
