## 2. Harmonic Motion

Given:

$$
x(t) = 0.2\cos(10\pi t)
$$

---

# Step 1 — Identify parameters

$$
A = 0.2, \quad \omega = 10\pi
$$

---

# Step 2 — Spring constant

$$
k = m\omega^2
$$

$$
k = 10 \cdot (10\pi)^2
$$

---

# Step 3 — Total energy

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2
$$
