## 12. Work and Energy (Constant Force)

Given:

$$
\vec{F}, \quad m
$$

---

# Step 1 — Acceleration

$$
\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{F}}{m}
$$

---

# Step 2 — Velocity

$$
\vec{v}(t) = \vec{v}_0 + \vec{a}t
$$

---

# Step 3 — Position

$$
\vec{r}(t) = \vec{r}_0 + \vec{v}_0 t + \frac{1}{2}\vec{a}t^2
$$

---

# Step 4 — Work

$$
W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d}
$$

---

# Step 5 — Check energy theorem

$$
W = \Delta KE
$$
