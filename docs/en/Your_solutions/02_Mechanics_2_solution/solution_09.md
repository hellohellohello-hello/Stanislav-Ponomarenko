## 9. Vertical Motion with Drag

Given:

$$
m\frac{dv}{dt} = -mg - kv
$$

---

# Step 1 — Solve differential equation

$$
v(t) = \left(v_0 + \frac{mg}{k}\right)e^{-kt/m} - \frac{mg}{k}
$$

---

# Step 2 — Maximum height

Occurs when:

$$
v = 0
$$

Solve for t, then substitute into position.
