## 5. Echo Ranging

Given:

$$
t = 1 \text{ s}, \quad v = 343 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

### Step 1 — Round trip

$$
d_{total} = vt = 343 \cdot 1 = 343 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Step 2 — One-way distance

$$
d = \frac{343}{2} = 171.5 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
d = 171.5 \text{ m}
$$
