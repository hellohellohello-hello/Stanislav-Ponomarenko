## 1. Wave Properties

Given:

$$
f = 440 \text{ Hz}, \quad v_{air} = 343 \text{ m/s}, \quad v_{water} = 1482 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

### Step 1 — Formula for wavelength

$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f}
$$

---

### Step 2 — Wavelength in air

$$
\lambda_{air} = \frac{343}{440} \approx 0.78 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Step 3 — Wavelength in water

$$
\lambda_{water} = \frac{1482}{440} \approx 3.37 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

- In air: **0.78 m**
- In water: **3.37 m**
