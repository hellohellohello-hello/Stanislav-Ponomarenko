## 7. Standing Wave Modes

Given:

$$
L = 0.8 \text{ m}, \quad n = 4
$$

---

### Step 1 — Formula

$$
L = \frac{n\lambda}{2}
$$

---

### Step 2 — Solve

$$
\lambda = \frac{2L}{n}
$$

$$
\lambda = \frac{2 \cdot 0.8}{4} = 0.4 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
\lambda = 0.4 \text{ m}
$$
