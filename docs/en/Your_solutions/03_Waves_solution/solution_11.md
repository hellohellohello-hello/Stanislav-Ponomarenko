## 11. Two-Slit Interference

Total wave:

$$
u = u_1 + u_2
$$

---

### Step 1 — Path difference

$$
\Delta r = r_1 - r_2
$$

---

### Step 2 — Condition

Constructive:

$$
\Delta r = n\lambda
$$

Destructive:

$$
\Delta r = \left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda
$$

---

### Final Explanation:

Interference pattern forms from superposition of two waves
