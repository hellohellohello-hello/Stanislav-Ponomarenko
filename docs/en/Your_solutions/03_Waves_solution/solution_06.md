## 6. Wave Equation

Given:

$$
y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)
$$

---

### Step 1 — Compare with standard

$$
y = A\sin(kx - \omega t)
$$

---

### Step 2 — Identify values

$$
A = 0.05, \quad k = 2\pi, \quad \omega = 50\pi
$$

---

### Step 3 — Wavelength

$$
\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = 1
$$

---

### Step 4 — Frequency

$$
f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = 25
$$

---

### Step 5 — Wave speed

$$
v = \frac{\omega}{k} = 25 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

- Amplitude: 0.05  
- Wavelength: 1 m  
- Frequency: 25 Hz  
- Speed: 25 m/s
