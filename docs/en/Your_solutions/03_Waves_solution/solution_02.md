## 2. String Harmonics

Given:

$$
L = 0.64 \text{ m}, \quad f = 330 \text{ Hz}
$$

---

### Step 1 — Fundamental wavelength

For fundamental mode:

$$
\lambda = 2L
$$

$$
\lambda = 2 \cdot 0.64 = 1.28 \text{ m}
$$

---

### Step 2 — Wave speed

$$
v = f \lambda
$$

$$
v = 330 \cdot 1.28 = 422.4 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
v = 422.4 \text{ m/s}
$$
