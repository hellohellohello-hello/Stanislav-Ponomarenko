## 3. Superposition Principle

Given:

$$
y_1 = A\sin(kx - \omega t), \quad y_2 = A\sin(kx + \omega t)
$$

---

### Step 1 — Use identity

$$
\sin a + \sin b = 2\sin\left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{a-b}{2}\right)
$$

---

### Step 2 — Apply

$$
y = 2A\sin(kx)\cos(\omega t)
$$

---

### Step 3 — Nodes

Nodes occur when:

$$
\sin(kx) = 0
$$

$$
kx = n\pi
$$

$$
x = \frac{n\pi}{k}
$$

---

### Final Answer:

Standing wave:

$$
y = 2A\sin(kx)\cos(\omega t)
$$

Nodes at:

$$
x = \frac{n\pi}{k}
$$
