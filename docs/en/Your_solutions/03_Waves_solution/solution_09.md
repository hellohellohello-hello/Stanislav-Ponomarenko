## 9. Damped Oscillator

Equation:

$$
m\ddot{x} + b\dot{x} + kx = 0
$$

---

### Step 1 — Characteristic equation

$$
mr^2 + br + k = 0
$$

---

### Step 2 — Cases

- Underdamped: $$b^2 < 4mk$$  
- Critically damped: $$b^2 = 4mk$$  
- Overdamped: $$b^2 > 4mk$$  

---

### Step 3 — General solution

Underdamped:

$$
x(t) = e^{-\gamma t}(A\cos\omega t + B\sin\omega t)
$$
